# Re

**Every rejection, logged and looked after.**
**每一封拒信，都被记录，也被好好安放。**

## What is Re? / Re 是什么？

Job hunting means collecting rejections — often more of them than offers.
Re is a simple place to keep track of every "no" you get: upload a
screenshot of the rejection email, paste in the message, or just type in
the company, position, and date. Re turns that pile of rejections into a
clear record instead of letting them get lost (or dreaded) in your inbox.

找工作的过程中，拒信往往比 offer 更多。Re 是一个简单的地方，帮你记录收到的
每一个「不」：可以上传拒信截图、粘贴邮件内容，或者直接手动填写公司名、
职位和日期。Re 把这些散落的拒信整理成一份清晰的记录，而不是让它们堆在
收件箱里，越看越让人焦虑。

## Why / 为什么做这个

Rejection is part of the process, not a verdict on your worth — but it
doesn't always feel that way in the moment. Re exists to make the process
visible and a little less lonely: see how far you've come, and get a small
word of encouragement along the way.

被拒绝是求职过程的一部分，不是对你价值的判定——但当下往往很难这么想。
Re 的意义在于让这个过程变得可见、没那么孤单：看到自己已经走了多远，
也在路上收到一点点鼓励。

## What it does / 它能做什么

- 📥 **Log a rejection three ways** — upload a screenshot (auto-read via
  OCR), paste the message text, or fill in the details manually.
  **三种方式记录拒信** — 上传截图（自动识别内容）、粘贴邮件文字，或手动
  填写信息。

- 📊 **See your dashboard** — how many companies have said no, and a
  timeline of your journey so far.
  **查看仪表盘** — 一共有多少家公司拒绝了你，以及你这一路走来的时间线。

- 💛 **Get a little support** — an encouraging note each time you log a
  rejection, plus a milestone message when you hit a meaningful number
  (your 1st, 5th, 10th, and beyond).
  **获得一点点支持** — 每次记录拒信都会收到一句鼓励，累计到有意义的
  节点（第 1、5、10 次……）还会收到专属的里程碑文案。
